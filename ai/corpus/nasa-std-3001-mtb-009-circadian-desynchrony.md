## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The medical technical brief describes circadian desynchrony: the body's internal clock falling out of step with external time cues. It lists spaceflight considerations such as altered light-dark cycles, irregular work schedules and confinement, and names effects including fatigue and slower or less accurate thinking. It describes education, sleep and fatigue assessment, and timing of light exposure.
