## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The page groups the challenges of human spaceflight into five hazards: space radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, gravity fields, and hostile or closed environments. It states that the hazards do not stand alone and can add to one another. NASA studies them in ground analogs, laboratories and on the International Space Station.
