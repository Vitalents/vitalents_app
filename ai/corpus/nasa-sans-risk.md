- [What are the top risks?] "This collection of eye and brain changes is called “spaceflight associated neuro-ocular syndrome,” or SANS."

## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

NASA describes SANS as a collection of eye and brain changes seen in some astronauts, linked to body fluids moving toward the head in weightlessness. Changes range from mild to clinically significant, and the risk is higher on longer missions. NASA studies ways to prevent the changes, including mechanical methods that counter the headward fluid shift and bed-rest studies on the ground.
