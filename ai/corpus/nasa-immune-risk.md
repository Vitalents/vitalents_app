## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

NASA describes how radiation, microgravity, poor sleep and other stressors can change the immune system of astronauts, and may trigger allergic reactions or reactivate dormant viruses. Research monitors blood, saliva and urine samples, including samples collected in flight. Some space station crew members have reported cold sores, rashes and atypical allergies.
