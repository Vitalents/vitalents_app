## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

NASA describes how altered gravity and moving between gravity levels may trigger motion sickness and spatial disorientation, which can make mission tasks harder. Research measures crew orientation after landing and tests training, orientation aids and recovery methods. The page states that most astronauts experience motion sickness during spaceflight.
