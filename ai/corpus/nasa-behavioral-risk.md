## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

NASA describes how long periods of isolation and confinement can raise the risk of behavioral issues, which may affect sleep, morale and decision making. A long Mars mission would add communication delays with family and the ground. Approaches NASA describes include self-care such as journaling, Earth-based isolation studies to test strategies, and privacy in the habitat.
