## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The University of Pennsylvania unit that developed Cognition describes it as a set of 10 short computerized tests built for astronauts and analog studies, covering a broader range of cognitive functions than the earlier battery. The page states the administration time after familiarization and that Cognition is part of NASA's Behavioral Health and Performance Standard Measures.
