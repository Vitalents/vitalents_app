## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The review summarises evidence from earlier missions of reduced sleep, disrupted circadian timing and lower performance in crew members, and the possible consequences for crew effectiveness and safety. It outlines NASA research on sleep and wake schedules and on the timing of countermeasures.
