- [p. 3, Crew Surgeon (CS) & Deputy Crew Surgeon (DCS)] "Monitor biomedical data, countermeasures, medical tests, experiments, and contingencies"
- [p. 3, Chief Medical Officer (CMO)] "Receives additional medical training to manage a variety of possible in mission health scenarios."

## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The technical brief summarises medical operations for spaceflight: crew health monitoring, the roles of the crew surgeon and the chief medical officer on board, and private medical and psychological conferences with flight surgeons. It states where medical decision-making authority lies and that each programme defines medical roles in its own requirements document.
