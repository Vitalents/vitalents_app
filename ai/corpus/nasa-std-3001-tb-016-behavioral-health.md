## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The technical brief summarises NASA-STD-3001 requirements and guidance on behavioral health and performance. It describes selection, training, in-flight support and monitoring, family support and return. For in-flight cognitive monitoring it describes comparing a monthly in-flight test with a preflight baseline, and it points to less obtrusive objective measures for longer missions.
