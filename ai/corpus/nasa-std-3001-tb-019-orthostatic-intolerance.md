## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The technical brief describes orthostatic intolerance: a reduced ability to keep blood pressure and blood flow to the brain when standing after return to gravity, which can progress to presyncope or syncope. In spaceflight, a headward fluid shift and a lower circulating fluid volume, together with pooling in the lower body on return, reduce venous return. Mitigation focuses on venous return and fluid volume.
