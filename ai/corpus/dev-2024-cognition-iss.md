## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The study reports Cognition Battery results from professional astronauts before, during and after 6-month International Space Station missions. Overall performance stayed largely stable, with slower performance on some tasks early in flight. The authors note a small sample and that the most acute transition phases of a mission were not measured.
