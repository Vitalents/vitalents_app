## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The technical brief summarises NASA-STD-3001 guidance on sleep accommodations for crew. It reports that astronauts sleep about six hours on average in space and describes the sleep environment crews need: control of light, noise and temperature, and private quarters on long missions. It also asks mission planners to schedule enough time for sleep.
