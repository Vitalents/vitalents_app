## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

The evidence report reviews whether heart rhythm disturbances occur in spaceflight. It states that the evidence is largely anecdotal or observational, with mixed findings from in-flight heart recordings on several programmes. It discusses fluid shifts and reduced cardiac mass as factors that need further study.
