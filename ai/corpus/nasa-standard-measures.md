## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

NASA describes Spaceflight Standard Measures, a consistent core set of measurements collected from crew members to follow how several body systems adapt to spaceflight. The set includes computer-based cognition tests, a sleep questionnaire after waking, activity monitoring, biological samples and a carotid artery ultrasound. The data are archived for researchers.
