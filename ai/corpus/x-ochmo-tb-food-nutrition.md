(p. 12, Space Food System Design: Mission Requirements)

(p. 19, Referenced Technical Requirements (NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 1 Rev C))

(p. 1, Executive Summary (fragment))

(p. 3, Background: Historical Events (fragment))
