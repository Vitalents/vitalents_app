## Neutral summary (written by the library agent from this source; not a quotation; needs review)

NASA describes stressors in spaceflight such as heavy workloads, irregular light-dark cycles and disrupted sleep, with possible short- and long-term health consequences for mission success. On the space station, LED lighting simulates Earth's light-dark cycle, and researchers study how light and caffeine use affect crew biological clocks. Crews are given designated time for relaxation and self-care.
