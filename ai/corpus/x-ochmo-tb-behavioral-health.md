# NASA-STD-3001 Technical Brief: Behavioral Health and Performance

Publisher: NASA Office of the Chief Health and Medical Officer (OCHMO)
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ochmo-tb-016-behavioral-health.pdf
Version: OCHMO-TB-016 Rev B
Published: 2023-10-24. Retrieved: 2026-11-14.
Library domain: General spaceflight context

## Verbatim excerpts

Every line below is quoted from the source as published (NASA work). Locators give the page or section.

> Crew behavioral health and performance are affected by missions in isolated, confined, and extreme (ICE) environments.
(p. 2, Executive Summary)

> Space flight occurs in an extreme environment that has unique stressors.
(p. 3, Background)

> It should be noted that current behavioral health support is provided before, during, and after the mission.
(p. 6, Application: Behavioral Health Support)

> Ensuring crew behavioral health over the long term is essential.
(p. 2, Executive Summary)

> Communication system should be provided in each private quarter
(p. 9, Habitat Design Guidance: Limited Communication with Home)

> Environmental factors such as noise, temperature, vibration, and light inhibit sleep and affect well-being in space.
(p. 8, Habitat Design Guidance: Lack of Sleep Quality and/or Quantity)
