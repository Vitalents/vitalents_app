# Vitalents · 2-minute video script

**Recording setup:** demo laptop, 1440 px browser width, S1 freshly loaded (**Settings** → **Load S1** → **Confirm**), link delay 24 min one way with **Speed up the demo** ticked, Wi-Fi off. Keep the "Simulated data · not a medical device" banner in every shot. Voice-over is about 270 words at a calm pace.

## Shot list and voice-over

| Time | Shot (screen · action) | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Vitalents lockup on the teal cover colour. Fade to **Today & map** (`/`), S1. | On a long mission, the astronaut is often the first to notice a change. And Earth is minutes away. |
| 0:08–0:20 | Map, slow zoom on the body figure. Cursor rests on heart, cognition, symptoms in turn. | This is Fahim, a fictional crew member on mission day 27. Vitalents shows each area with three separate labels: is the data usable, does it need attention, and has the message reached Earth. Never one colour. |
| 0:20–0:32 | Click **cognition** → domain card **Now** tab: result against the personal range, "Frozen". | Fahim is compared with his own past results, not with a population. While this follow-up is open, his reference is frozen, so a lower result never becomes his new reference. |
| 0:32–0:50 | **Check & Decode** (`/check`) → **Go to Decode** → **Demo length (30 s)** ticked → a few seconds of the round → **Message from Earth** → result beside the self-rating. Speed up the round footage, label "sped up". | Decode is a 90-second task, inspired by a well-known symbol test. Our version needs independent validation. Missed and late answers are never counted as wrong. The result sits beside his self-rating of 8 out of 10. Two scales, never combined. |
| 0:50–1:05 | **Today's review** (`/review`) → **Why?** opens: records, rule, limits. | Every finding opens its data, its rule and its limits. Lower results and short nights happen together. Vitalents shows two observations, not a cause. |
| 1:05–1:20 | **Plan & follow-ups** → **Compare schedule options** → **Now** vs **With the change** → **Request this change**. | One step at a time, with its source and who decides. Moving a meeting gives a longer rest opportunity tonight. The planner approves, and sees only this request. |
| 1:20–1:35 | **Sleep** card → **Understand** → cited answer; then the dose question → refusal → **Add this question for review**. Show Wi-Fi off icon. | Offline, the onboard assistant answers from a NASA library, citing every sentence. It refuses medication and dose questions, and logs what it cannot answer. |
| 1:35–1:50 | **Review & sharing** (`/share`) → **Check what will be sent** → **Send** → delivery moves to **Delivered** → **Medical team view** → **Mark reviewed** → back to astronaut: **Reviewed**. | Fahim sees exactly what is shared. Private notes never leave. The summary waits honestly through the delay, and "Reviewed" appears only when the medical team reads it. |
| 1:50–2:00 | Lockup + "vitalents.life" + "Simulated data · advisory, not diagnostic". | Vitalents. Health follow-up the astronaut understands and acts on. Advisory, not diagnostic. |
