# Sources and credits

## Scientific context

The [source catalog](../content/SOURCES.md) lists the 34 resources used by the library, with titles, publishers, dates and intended use. NASA Human Research Program material supplies background for the domain map, sleep, cognition, cardiovascular and other spaceflight risks. NASA technical briefs provide explanatory context. They are not astronaut measurements and do not endorse Vitalents or establish its numeric thresholds.

Other sources include ESA radiation context, the University of Pennsylvania Cognition battery, and the Basner and Dev research papers listed in the catalog. Their findings do not transfer validation to this custom Decode implementation. The [verification record](../ai/source_verification.json) retains source hashes, excerpt checks, locator corrections and rights uncertainty. All catalog entries remain draft pending source review; quotation matching alone is not clinical approval or redistribution clearance.

LifeSnaps v4 is credited as a candidate for separate descriptive evaluation, not as the source of the current synthetic research results. See the [dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/7229547) and [dataset paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01764-x). The current application does not demonstrate measured performance on LifeSnaps or real astronaut records.

## AI and media disclosure

AI coding tools assisted implementation, tests and documentation. The optional application assistant uses Ollama with `gpt-oss:20b` and `nomic-embed-text`; public Cloudflare builds use reviewed recorded answers. Raw recordings and the [publication review policy](../ai/cache_review.json) remain available, including the reasons two unsupported explanations were withheld.

The fictional family message in Decode uses an AI-generated portrait and synthetic speech. It does not portray a real family or clone a real person's voice. The [media provenance record](media-provenance.json) identifies the image provider, speech provider and voice, transcript and asset paths. Neither asset is NASA footage or a mission recording.

Vitalents uses its own Human V identity. Font assets and relevant licence notices are retained with their packages and the [brand assets](../app/public/brand/), including the Bricolage Grotesque licence. Third-party libraries retain their own licences; their use does not grant a blanket licence to every source excerpt or dataset.

This repository does not establish official submission, event eligibility, challenge-resource acceptance or NASA endorsement. The exact qualifying resource and final presentation requirements still require external confirmation.
