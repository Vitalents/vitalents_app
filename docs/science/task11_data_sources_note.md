## 1. LifeSnaps v4 — Terrestrial human research

- **Provenance and identifier.** Yfantidou et al., EU Horizon 2020 RAIS project (grant 813162). The de-identified release comprises data from 71 adult research participants living on Earth. It combines Fitbit Sense records, ecological momentary assessments and surveys. Zenodo lists version 4 as open and provides `rais_anonymized.zip` with CSV exports and MongoDB dumps. The version DOI is `10.5281/zenodo.7229547`. [Zenodo v4 record](https://zenodo.org/records/7229547), [Scientific Data paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9622868/).
- **Metrics and use.** Task 2 mapped daily/hourly Fitbit and SEMA exports plus STAI and PANAS CSVs. Use recorded sleep interval (`sleep_duration`, ms) and estimated minutes asleep (`minutesAsleep`, min) as separate fields. Use heart rate (`bpm`) and resting heart rate (`resting_hr`, bpm) as distinct measures, with aggregation retained. Steps are an activity count. Weekly STAI and PANAS scores can be displayed descriptively as self-reports. These support the proposed Real Data Mode, sleep and heart Body Map cards, recorded activity and research trends, and same-person descriptive reference history. [Fitbit sleep field definitions](https://dev.fitbit.com/build/reference/web-api/sleep/get-sleep-log-by-date/).
- **Limits.** The Task 2 audit reports 3,530 daily participant records with both `sleep_duration` and `bpm`, with 58 of 71 participants having at least 14 such days. There were 191 matching dates across 44 participants when both weekly surveys were also required. These are prior audit totals, and the participant CSVs were unavailable for recalculation in this handoff. Local hour has an undisclosed UTC offset and day-level joins cannot establish clock-time alignment. Preserve missingness and observed zero. The audit found 70 excess hourly rows on repeated natural keys, including one conflicting duplicate. Keep the source locator and flag conflicts. Daily skin conductance was 94.87% missing and observed in two participants, so it cannot support core stress monitoring. Export-specific temperature units and signed-field semantics are **NOT VERIFIED**. Skin temperature cannot stand in for core body temperature. This cohort supports descriptive terrestrial analysis and supplies no astronaut norms, DECODE scores or diagnostic thresholds. See Task 2 `Vitalents_Task2_LifeSnaps_Audit.docx` and `Vitalents_LifeSnaps_Audit_Details.json`.
- **Access and rights.** The paper explicitly describes open research use and asks users to protect confidentiality, cite the source, and avoid re-identification, participant contact and advertising. The paper itself is CC BY 4.0. The accessible Zenodo record shows an open download and a License heading with no readable license value. The exact license for the v4 archive and public redistribution rights for raw or participant-level mapped files remain **NOT VERIFIED**. The project use documented here is attributed descriptive research with protected records. The handoff includes no participant files. [Zenodo rights record](https://zenodo.org/records/7229547#rights), [Authors’ usage notes and article license](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9622868/).

Generated values, approvals and share previews are fictional. They establish no empirical distribution, intervention effect, clinical threshold or real communication event. ## 3. Live DECODE measurements

- **Provenance and identifier.** Intended first-party measurements from the current player’s 90-second symbol–digit task. Task 2 defines `decode_session` and `decode_response_event`. `science.decode.verify` consumes a versioned key registry, session context, response rows and an independently captured ordered ID seal. The delivered examples are synthetic.
- **Metrics, use and components.** Store stimulus and response timing (monotonic ms), accepted/correct/incorrect counts, accuracy (% of accepted responses), median correct RT (ms), and missed/late/duplicate/interrupted event counts. Preserve version, device/input, UTC context, interruption and practice state. These support M3 game feedback, M4 descriptive review and compatible within-player reference comparisons. An undefined score remains unavailable. Practice and interrupted/unverified sessions stay outside references.
- **Access, rights and limits.** The project intends use for the player’s feedback and role-scoped follow-up. Live data, permissions, consent basis, retention, research/export rights and any public license are **NOT VERIFIED**. Device timing, focus, protocol and practice can affect observed performance. A task score measures game performance. Clinical, fatigue, fitness-for-duty and operational-readiness interpretations require independent evidence.

## 4. Application-generated records

- **Provenance and identifier.** Vitalents UI/services and engines create `application_generated` records under the proposed Task 2 contract. Representative entities include `information_status`, `personal_reference`, `daily_review`, `finding`, `action`, `follow_up`, `sharing_permission`, `share_package` and `sync_event`.
- **Metrics, use and components.** Quality categories and reasons, reference counts/deltas/versions, rule and approval links, action/follow-up states, and storage/send/delivery/review timestamps support the Body Map, daily review, audit trail, scheduling, consultation, permissions and offline continuity. Keep derived values linked to original records and preserve fictional provenance for outputs derived from the replay. A computed rest window and a sync status describe workflow state or opportunity.
- **Access, rights and limits.** The proposed access model gives the astronaut a share preview, the specialist a scoped consult record and the planner only authorized operational information. Intended use is in-app workflow and explicitly scoped sharing. Application enforcement, deployed access/retention terms and public redistribution rights are **NOT VERIFIED**. The handoff contains fixture projections and an engine, with no operational records or verified delivery.
